# Resetting your password

In case you need to reset your forgotten password:

1. On the Matcha.io sign-in screen, click *Forgot your password?*.

2. Enter your email address, then click *Send code*.

   A recovery code will be sent to your email.

3. Enter the recovery code, then click *Next*.

4. Enter your new password, then click *Confirm*.

## Password requirements

- Minimum 12 characters
- A combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, and at least one number and one symbol
- Must not be a previous password
