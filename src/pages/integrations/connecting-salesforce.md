# Connecting Salesforce

## Overview

In Matcha.io Portal, go to **Integrations → Salesforce → Connect**. Sign in to the Salesforce organization and approve the requested OAuth permissions.

## Recommended steps

Select the Salesforce objects and events to synchronize, map required fields, and run **Test connection**. Save and enable the integration after a successful test.

## Additional guidance

An authorized Salesforce administrator may need to approve the connected app. If authorization fails, check browser pop-up settings and Salesforce access policies.
