# Updating payment details

## Overview

A workspace billing administrator can open **Settings → Billing → Payment methods** and select **Update payment method**.

## Recommended steps

Enter the new payment details in the secure payment form and confirm the update. Review the billing contact email and any outstanding invoices.

## Additional guidance

Only billing administrators can change payment methods. Do not send card details to support by email or chat.

## Contact support

If you still need help, provide the article ID **KB-BILL-001**, a description of the problem, relevant UTC timestamps, and sanitized request or execution IDs. Never share passwords, access tokens, or payment-card details.
